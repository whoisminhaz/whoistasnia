# Who Is Tasnia (The 23rd Chapter) 🌙

> A mobile-first, emotionally deep, and spiritually grounded 23rd birthday tribute for **Tasnia Yeasmin Suraiya (Tasu)**.  
> Conceived and authored with devotion by **Minhaz** from Alor Setar, Kedah, Malaysia to Sylhet, Bangladesh.

---

## 🕊️ Dedication & Historical Anchors

- **Her Birth**: Sylhet, Bangladesh • Friday, October 31, 2003 (6 Ramadan 1424 AH / 16 Kartik 1410 BS)
- **The Celestial Sky**: 42% Waxing Crescent Moon
- **The Sacred Day**: Born on *Sayyidul Ayyam* (The First Friday) of the blessed month of Ramadan
- **The Historic Anchor**: On the exact date of her birth, Malaysia witnessed a historic 22-year transition of leadership. Years later, that very soil became the sanctuary where Minhaz studies, works, and prays for their shared future.
- **The Horizon**: 2,276 km across the Bay of Bengal ➔ 0 km (Under One Roof / Nikah, Insha'Allah).

---

## 🌟 Architectural & Design Highlights

1. **Strict Mobile-First Viewport**: Engineered specifically for high-end mobile displays (390px – 430px) with elegant responsive centering for desktop displays.
2. **Royal Islamic & Antique Palette**:
   - Deep Midnight Emerald / Dark Charcoal (`#0F1715` / `#090F0E`)
   - Warm Parchment Accents (`#F5F0E6`)
   - Antique Muted Gold Highlights (`#C5A059`)
   - Subtle geometric Khatim & arabesque watermarks
3. **Sections**:
   - **The Gateway Envelope**: Waxing crescent seal opening her 23rd chapter.
   - **The Day You Arrived**: Live running counter of Years, Months, Weeks, Total Days, Total Hours, and live-ticking Seconds from Oct 31, 2003 (Sylhet Time, UTC+6).
   - **Historical Context Deck**: First Friday of Ramadan 1424 AH, 16 Kartik 1410 (Hemanta season in Sylhet), 42% Waxing Crescent Moon, and the Malaysian leadership coincidence.
   - **The Art Gallery Photo & Audio Player**: Museum-framed sepia-gold photo canvas with custom upload capability, paired with a custom voice-note audio player featuring animated gold waveforms and built-in acoustic tone synthesis.
   - **The 2,276 KM Bridge**: Alor Setar, Malaysia (6.14° N, 100.39° E) to Sylhet, Bangladesh (24.89° N, 91.87° E) with dual live ticking time zone clocks (MYT / BST) and milestone timeline.
   - **Verses for Our Future**: 5 Interactive Reflection Cards featuring English and Bengali side-by-side toggles with personal notes:
     * *Surah Al-Asr (103:1-3)*
     * *Surah Ar-Rum (30:21) & Al-Baqarah (2:187)*
     * *Sahih Muslim (1467)*
     * *Surah Al-Baqarah (2:153) & Ash-Sharh (94:5-6)*
     * *Rumi-Inspired Soul Reflection*
   - **Closing Du'a & Interactive Ameen**: A heartfelt supplication for her 23rd year with interactive Ameen button, golden confetti explosion, and the revealed dedication message.

Card 1 • The Sanctity of Time Al-Asr 103:1-3
وَالْعَصْرِ ۝ إِنَّ الْإِنسَانَ لَفِي خُسْرٍ ۝ إِلَّا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا بِالصَّبْرِ
“By time, indeed, mankind is in loss, except for those who have believed and done righteous deeds and advised each other to truth and advised each other to patience.”
“সময়ের শপথ! নিশ্চয় মানুষ ক্ষতির মধ্যে নিমজ্জিত। কিন্তু তারা নয়, যারা ঈমান এনেছে, সৎকাজ করেছে এবং পরস্পরকে সত্যের উপদেশ দিয়েছে ও ধৈর্যের উপদেশ দিয়েছে।”
Minhaz's Note: Time is not our enemy, Tasu. Every single day of distance is an investment in Sabr, teaching us that what is built on truth and patience will stand firm for a lifetime.
Card 2 • Sakinah & Garments Ar-Rum 30:21 • Al-Baqarah 2:187
وَمِنْ آيَاتِهِ أَنْ خَلَقَ لَكُم مِّنْ أَنفُسِكُمْ أَزْوَاجًا لِّتَسْكُنُوا إِلَيْهَا وَجَعَلَ بَيْنَكُم مَّوَدَّةً وَرَحْمَةً ... هُنَّ لِبَاسٌ لَّكُمْ وَأَنتُمْ لِبَاسٌ لَّهُنَّ
“And of His signs is that He created for you mates that you may find tranquility in them; and He placed between you affection and mercy... They are clothing for you and you are clothing for them.”
“এবং তাঁর নিদর্শনাবলীর মধ্যে রয়েছে যে, তিনি তোমাদের মধ্য হতেই সৃষ্টি করেছেন তোমাদের সঙ্গিনী যাতে তোমরা তাদের কাছে প্রশান্তি পাও এবং তোমাদের মাঝে পারস্পরিক ভালোবাসা ও দয়া সৃষ্টি করেছেন... তারা তোমাদের পোশাক এবং তোমরা তাদের পোশাক।”
Minhaz's Note: You are my tranquility (Sakinah). To be your garment means to shelter your dreams, protect your honor, and keep your gentle heart warm whenever the world feels cold.
Card 3 • The Priceless Treasure Sahih Muslim 1467
الدُّنْيَا مَتَاعٌ وَخَيْرُ مَتَاعِ الدُّنْيَا الْمَرْأَةُ الصَّالِحَةُ
“The whole world is a pleasure, and the best treasure of this world is the righteous woman.”
“সমগ্র পৃথিবীটাই পার্থিব সম্পদ, আর এই পৃথিবীর সর্বশ্রেষ্ঠ সম্পদ হলো এক পুণ্যময়ী নারী।”
Minhaz's Note: Your integrity, your quiet dignity, and your deep faith make you the highest gift Allah has brought into my life. That is why waiting and striving for you is an honor.
Card 4 • Ease After Hardship Ash-Sharh 94:5-6
يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اسْتَعِينُوا بِالصَّبْرِ وَالصَّلَاةِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَ الصَّابِرِينَ ... فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا ۝ إِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا
“O you who have believed, seek help through patience and prayer. Indeed, Allah is with the patient... For indeed, with hardship comes ease. Indeed, with hardship comes ease.”
“হে মুমিনগণ, তোমরা ধৈর্য ও নামাজের মাধ্যমে সাহায্য প্রার্থনা করো। নিশ্চয়ই আল্লাহ ধৈর্যশীলদের সাথে আছেন... অতএব কষ্টের সাথেই তো রয়েছে স্বস্তি। নিশ্চয় কষ্টের সাথেই স্বস্তি আছে।”
Minhaz's Note: Notice how Allah repeated it twice. The difficulties and the distance are not punishments; they are the fertile soil where our future ease and union are taking root.

A Prayer for Tasnia’s 23rd Year

O Allah, bless Tasnia Yeasmin Suraiya as she steps into her 23rd year. Envelope her soul in unwaivering peace, grant radiant health to her body, and protect her tender heart from the sorrows and betrayals of this world.

Bless her hands and her imagination with boundless creative barakah; make her art a source of joy and elevation. Protect her parents, honor her sacrifices, and grant her ease in every silent hardship she bears.

And Ya Rabb, bridge our 2,276 kilometers in Your divine timing. Unite our hearts under Your shade, bless our intentions with Nikah, and grant us a home filled with laughter, mercy, and devotion to You.
